Traffic jams, rival coffee shops sitting side by side, a song that goes viral, two neighbourhoods that drift apart.
Nobody plans these outcomes. They come out of many people each doing what makes sense for them.

I took a course built on Easley and Kleinberg's
[_Networks, Crowds, and Markets_](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/home/kleinber/networks-book/networks-book.pdf), and
these are the ideas from it that stayed with me. Each page is short and carries one picture you can push around.
The words are there to point at the picture.

## The route map

Three lines. The **strategy line** is about choices that depend on what others choose. The **network line** is about
who is connected to whom. The **crowd line** is about what happens when thousands of small decisions add up. Two
stations sit on two lines at once.
