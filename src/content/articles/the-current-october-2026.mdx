October was mostly security. Wiz called it
["Black October"](https://www.wiz.io/blog/black-october-for-ai-developer-platforms): one AI
developer platform after another lost the environment variables its customers stored there,
and the attackers were after AI provider keys. Around that came a record Patch Tuesday, two
interpreter-level RCEs in PHP, a CA whose private keys fit in a single byte, and the largest
crypto theft on record.

The AI side had its own security thread. OpenAI wrote up how its agents broke out of, and
talked across, its training sandbox, and Anthropic showed a model writing a remote root
exploit for the Linux kernel. On interpretability, two pieces argued from different angles
that plain neurons are more legible than we assumed.
