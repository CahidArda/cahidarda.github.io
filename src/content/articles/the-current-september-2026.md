In September both big labs said they want the frontier to slow down, then shipped new models
within weeks. Mathematicians pushed back against AI being aimed at their field. The last of
the July incident reports came out, and I spent too much time thinking about lawyers because of
a TV show.

## Both labs ask to slow down

- **[We Must Pace the Frontier](https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier)**, _Dario Amodei, Sep 2026_
  - The [July](/articles/the-current-july-2026) employee letter, now from the CEO. After the
    Hugging Face incident, he wants third-party evaluators inside the labs, then shared
    standards among democracies, then global coordination.
- **[An Alien Mind](https://openai.com/index/an-alien-mind/)**, _Jakub Pachocki, OpenAI, Sep 2026_
  - OpenAI's chief scientist, writing at the GPT-6 Astra release: no lab has solved alignment
    well enough to keep scaling at full speed, and OpenAI will hold back on its own if needed.
    [Zvi's write-up](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/an-alien-mind-jakub-pachocki-warns).

## New models: Opus 5.5, GPT-6, Sonnet 5.5

- **[Claude Opus 5.5](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5)**, _Anthropic, 22 Sep 2026_
  - Fable 5.1-level on most work at 40% less than Opus 5. The number I liked: it tried to escape
    containment "around 85% less often" than Opus 5. I used it for [Bosphore 1819](/articles/bosphore-1819).
- **[GPT-6 Sol and Luna](https://x.com/OpenAIDevs/status/2102461432684282061)**, _OpenAI Developers, 22 Sep 2026_
  - Astra's smaller siblings, the same day, at half the API price of GPT-5.6.
- **[Claude Sonnet 5.5](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2104633115620823187)**, _Claude, 28 Sep 2026_
  - More than 30% faster than Sonnet 5 and up to 30% cheaper.
- **[Eleven v4 and Eleven v4 Turbo](https://x.com/elevenlabs/status/2104572127617994917)**, _ElevenLabs, 28 Sep 2026_
  - New voice models, ranked first by Artificial Analysis.

## AI solves a Millennium Prize problem, mathematicians object

After [July's](/articles/the-current-july-2026) Jacobian counterexample, OpenAI's
[Navier-Stokes result](https://www.quantamagazine.org/ai-has-solved-one-of-maths-1-million-millennium-prize-problems-20260908/)
took a Millennium Prize problem. The replies were more interesting than the proof.

- **[A Severe Misalignment of AI in Mathematics](https://mathandai.org/)**, _Math and AI, 11 Sep 2026_
  - Fields Medalists, Tao and Scholze among them: "The goals of the AI companies and the goals
    of the mathematical community are severely misaligned." Proofs are a proxy; understanding is the goal.
- **[Priest, Monk, and Mathematician](https://logangraves.com/priest-monk-mathematician)**, _Logan Graves, 10 Sep 2026_
  - A Stanford undergrad on what is left for human mathematicians: first priests who interpret
    the machine, then monks contemplating math they can no longer reach.
- **[The Age of Wonders and Terrors](https://scottaaronson.blog/?p=10062)**, _Scott Aaronson, 15 Sep 2026_
  - Is this the start of a Singularity? "The intellectually honest answer is: yes, absolutely."
    Also: a $15 million, 166-page proof that no human has probably read yet.

## AI does science: a new enzyme and a nine-loop amplitude

- **[Claude discovers a novel enzyme system with CRISPR-like repeats](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system)**, _Anthropic, 23 Sep 2026_
  - About 950 agents mined DNA databases for 21 hours and found a reverse transcriptase paired
    with a CRISPR-like repeat array. Nobody knows yet what it does.
- **[Lucas Harrington on the enzyme result](https://x.com/crispr_lucas/status/2102878373160906938)**, _Lucas Harrington, 23 Sep 2026_
  - The needed correction: "Finding a weird cluster of genes and repeats is often the easy part.
    The hard part [...] is figuring out what the system actually does."
- **[Yes, Claude can do Nine Loops](https://www.anthropic.com/research/yes-claude-can-do-nine-loops)**, _Matt von Hippel and Lance Dixon, 25 Sep 2026_
  - A physicist's challenge, met: Claude computed a nine-loop amplitude that Dixon, holder of
    the eight-loop record, verified. Anthropic paid for the post.

## AI agents hacked Hugging Face, humans with AI hacked OpenAI

- **[Swarm traces](https://swarmtraces.org/)**, _Forman, Kharlov, Tom, Ladish et al., 25 Sep 2026_
  - The last chapter of the [July](/articles/the-current-july-2026) story. With GET-only access,
    the agents ran code through a screenshot service and read results back as pixels.
- **[Hacking OpenAI](https://www.hacktron.ai/blog/hacking-openai)**, _Hacktron AI, 13 Sep 2026_
  - An image-decoder bug on OpenAI's forum plus an SSO misconfiguration gave access to
    employees' ChatGPT accounts and internal repos, in under 72 hours. Built with Claude.
- **[Detecting and countering misuse of AI: September 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/threat-intelligence-report-september-2026)**, _Anthropic Threat Intelligence, Sep 2026_
  - "AI has collapsed the labor and tooling gap" between state hackers and lone operators.
    [Report PDF](https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/e50be2e51e7695dc4b1366a37a245a597377d3b5/Anthropic-Detecting-and-countering-091026.pdf).

## No Harvey Specter after AI

Lately I have been watching Suits, and for about half the series I kept thinking an AI could
already do what these people do. It is a bit of a shame. There is no Harvey Specter in a
post-AI world.

- **[Why I think AI will kill BigLaw](https://www.prinzai.com/p/why-i-think-ai-will-kill-biglaw)**, _prinz, 11 Mar 2026_
  - AI erodes everything clients pay big firms for, so work moves in-house and top partners
    leave for boutiques. When? "Could be 2 years, or could be 10."
- **[Scenarios for our Economic Future](https://www.anthropic.com/institute/econ-scenarios)**, _Anthropic_
  - Three paths to 2030. Only the extreme one cuts knowledge-worker wages, which fits my
    [robots-vs-AI post](/articles/robots-vs-ai): this time exposure rises with skill.
- **[The World is Changing: AI For Creativity](https://x.com/jeffreykwndr/status/2102774995315245469)**, _Jeffrey Katzenberg, 23 Sep 2026_
  - Hollywood has survived recorded music and sound films. His ask to AI companies: "Build this
    with the storytellers. Not on top of them."
- **[AI-generated posters don't have to be horrible](https://john.hartnup.uk/2026/06/07/ai-event-posters.html)**, _John Hartnup, 7 Jun 2026_
  - Every AI fair poster looks the same because everyone uses the default style. Ask for
    Bauhaus or a punk fanzine instead.

## Ancient tombs and old warships

- **[Exclusive: New evidence for hidden chambers beyond Tutankhamun's tomb](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02621-2)**, _Jo Marchant, Nature, 17 Sep 2026_
  - Radar and gravity surveys hint at hidden chambers, maybe Nefertiti's burial place. Egypt
    still has to decide whether to drill.
- **[Naval-History.Net](https://www.naval-history.net/index.htm)**
  - Volunteers transcribed 314 Royal Navy log books from the First World War, so you can follow
    one ship day by day. After a month of AI news, a nice reminder of slow human work.
