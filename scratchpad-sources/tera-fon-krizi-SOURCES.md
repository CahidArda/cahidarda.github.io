## Deliberately NOT claimed in the post

- **Tera's funds' exact share of DSTKF free float.** Widely reported at 39.41% but not retrieved from
  a KAP/MKK filing. The post instead uses TLY's own disclosed portfolio weight against DSTKF's
  disclosed free float, which is verifiable arithmetic from two primary-ish sources, and says so.
- **Any claim that manipulation is established.** Charges are charges. The post says so explicitly.
- **Albanian 1997 figures.** imf.org was unreachable; the Jarvis paper's numbers are unverified, so
  Albania is not used.
- **Any aggregate ₺ figure for the 131 funds.** SPK published the investor count, not the total AUM.
- **Precise arrest dates for individuals where sources disagree.** The post gives the week, not the day,
  except where a single source is explicit (Kilimci).
